FEATURED COAST

Antalya: where to stay

Turkey's Mediterranean coast is a longtime favorite with Russian and European travelers alike, prized for its all-inclusive resorts and beach clubs.

Read moreRead less

Konyaaltı and Lara Beach anchor the big resort strip, with pools, spas and private beach access built into most stays; the Old Town (Kaleiçi) offers a quieter, boutique alternative inside a restored harbor quarter. Peak season runs June through September, so book resort-front rooms early if you want direct beach access.

FEATURED CITY

Tokyo: where to stay

Tokyo rewards travelers who pick a base close to a train line. Shinjuku puts you near nightlife and the fastest transit connections; Asakusa trades some convenience for a quieter, more traditional feel near the city's oldest temple.

Read moreRead less

Wherever you land, book early — central Tokyo hotels fill up fast during cherry blossom season and the autumn foliage weeks. Shibuya and Shinjuku suit first-time visitors who want nightlife and easy transit; Asakusa and Ueno reward travelers chasing a slower, more traditional pace.
