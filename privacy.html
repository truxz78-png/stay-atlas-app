Privacy Policy for Stay Atlas

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Stay Atlas ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") is an affiliate travel discovery application that helps users find hotels, vacation rentals, car rentals, and travel guides through links to third-party travel booking platforms. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with your use of the App.

1. Information We Collect

Stay Atlas does not require you to create an account. Stay Atlas does not require you to log in. The App itself does not collect personal information, such as your name, email address, or payment details.

The App does not use analytics tools and does not collect usage statistics. Limited technical data may be received by the third-party services described in Section 7.

2. Third-Party Affiliate Links

Stay Atlas contains affiliate links to third-party travel service providers, such as Booking.com, Agoda, Expedia, Hotels.com, Vrbo, Trip.com, intui.travel, Kayak, and GetYourGuide. When you tap on any of these links, you will be taken to the third party's website or app, which operates under its own privacy policy and terms of service, entirely independent of Stay Atlas.

We do not control these third-party providers. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, content, or actions. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any third-party site before providing any personal or payment information.

3. Advertising Identifier

Stay Atlas does not use the Advertising ID and does not display in-app advertisements.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not rent personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties. This is because the App itself does not collect personal information. Any data shared with a third-party travel provider occurs directly between you and that provider once you leave the App via an affiliate link.

5. Children's Privacy

Stay Atlas is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data Security

Stay Atlas does not collect personal user data. Stay Atlas does not store personal user data on its own servers. For this reason, there is no personal data at risk of being compromised through the App itself.

7. Technical Services and Local Storage

Stay Atlas does not use analytics. Stay Atlas does not use tracking cookies. Stay Atlas does not use user accounts. However, the App and this website rely on a few technical services that may receive limited technical data, such as your IP address and browser information, when you use them:

Hosting. The App's content is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs under GitHub's own privacy statement.

Fonts. Text fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, a service operated by Google.

Automatic translation. To show the App in your language, the text of the page is sent to Google's translation service. You do not enter or send any personal information for this purpose.

Local storage. The App saves your chosen language on your own device (browser local storage) so it can remember it next time. This setting is never sent to us, and you can remove it by clearing the App's storage.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
