Tokyo: where to stay

Tokyo rewards travelers who pick a base close to a train line. Shinjuku puts you near nightlife and the fastest transit connections; Asakusa trades some convenience for a quieter, more traditional feel near the city's oldest temple. Wherever you land, book early — central Tokyo hotels fill up fast during cherry blossom season and the autumn foliage weeks.
